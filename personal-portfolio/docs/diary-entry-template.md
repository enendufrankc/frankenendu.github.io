# Monthly diary template

Use the author workspace at `/diary/admin/`. Start a new month, choose a short title and private summary, then use these prompts. Drafts stay visible only to the owner. Keep source notes and actual entries outside git.

## The month in a paragraph

What changed? What was the central question? If the month is unfinished, state the evidence cutoff.

## My contribution

What did I personally build, decide or improve? Distinguish my work from the team's work and credit collaborators.

## Across R&D

Which team developments connect to the work? Explain why they matter to an external reader without reproducing internal plans or operational details.

## One decision worth exploring

The problem, options considered, trade-offs and decision. Include the discarded option only when it explains the choice.

## What the evidence says

State dated observations and limitations. Distinguish a prototype, a deployed service, adoption and demonstrated impact. Add only attachments suitable for invited external readers.

## What I learned

What will I carry forward or do differently?

## Looking ahead

Questions and intentions for next month, rather than unconfirmed delivery promises.

Before publishing, check personal attribution, external sharing suitability, dates, claims, links and attached files. Public previews are optional: enabling one exposes the title, month and teaser to anyone.
